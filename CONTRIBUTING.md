Learn how to export your own posts, carousels, and reels, keep captions and timestamps, download media in original quality, and build a searchable folder — with runnable Python and a legal-use checklist. Built for creators, social media managers, and anyone who wants their own content backed up. **Completely free.** No signup. No paywall. No hidden chapters.

Most "Instagram downloader" tutorials scrape other people's content. **This guide archives your own posts** — images, carousels, reels, captions, and timestamps — into a clean, searchable folder, so your media survives account changes and platform shifts.
